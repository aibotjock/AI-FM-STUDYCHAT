This app is being prepared and privately tested. These disclosures describe the current implementation; they are not a declaration of readiness for a public store release.

The operator’s public contact is not configured. Public release is blocked until the operator, support arrangements, and final disclosures are completed.

Board exam study only — no medical advice or clinical use

Family Medicine Board Study is an educational tool solely for family medicine board exam learning and preparation. It is not a medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, triage or emergency service. Do not use its output to make decisions for a real patient. Original board-style practice questions are not official ABFM questions and the app is not affiliated with or endorsed by ABFM.

Guideline summaries are original paraphrases with official reference links. Source checks and monthly change monitoring do not guarantee completeness, clinical accuracy or clinician review. Review status and source-check dates are shown with study material. AI replies may be incomplete or incorrect even when they cite a source.

What the app keeps

Your study cards, review ratings, board practice sessions, answers and optional confidence recorded before feedback, conversations, accepted AI-review records, voice and learning preferences, and study self-reflections are saved on the app’s server. Technical speech request records help prevent repeating paid requests; they contain request status and bounded metadata, not audio files. In account mode, it also stores your email, a password hash, authentication sessions, coaching usage records, and any answer reports you submit. Your password is hashed rather than saved as readable text.

Learning priorities are derived on the server from the last 32 retained trusted practice sessions. They are study signals, not competence ratings. Confidence is optional; leaving it blank does not prevent practice. Imported summaries do not establish verified learning evidence. This learning-plan computation makes no AI request and does not export your answers or confidence to Ingenium.

In subscription mode, Google Play purchase information is used to verify your access. Purchase tokens are encrypted on the server when billing is configured. The app uses an account identifier to associate an eligible purchase with your study account.

When information leaves the app

This pilot uses OpenAI for natural conversational coaching; Claude is inactive. A usual generated reply involves two paid text requests: one writes the reply and a separate request reviews its factual claims against the included study sources. Both receive a limited recent conversation history, saved learning preferences and relevant source-linked study summaries; the review also receives the draft reply. The reviewer quotes each factual claim and selects its supporting current study section. The app uses fixed source references to supply the original excerpt rather than asking the model to copy it, then saves the accepted text and review record with your conversation. New review records fingerprint the complete supporting study sections so a changed section cannot be replayed as the same checked evidence. Older records retain their earlier checks. This automated review can miss an error and is not clinician approval or an accuracy guarantee. Your own statements and past conversation are context, not independent medical evidence. Rejected drafts are withheld without an automatic retry or model fallback. Direct original quiz selection and answer grading do not require a model request. Provider selection and credentials are managed on the server.

The configured AI provider and model can be checked in Study preferences. Provider records follow that provider’s current contract, retention policies, and account settings; account deletion in this app does not itself delete records held by an AI provider.

Google Play handles purchase and subscription transactions. This app receives purchase information needed for verification; it does not collect your card payment details.

Hands-free conversational study voice captures short microphone turns while the page is visible, including when Coach is preparing or playing a reply. Your browser is asked to apply echo cancellation and noise suppression; actual speaker and Bluetooth performance depends on the device and needs phone verification. After a detected turn ends, the app sends a bounded recording through its authenticated server to OpenAI's fixed transcription model. It does not provide permanent API credentials to the browser or send audio to an unrestricted Realtime conversation. Recognized text follows the same conversation generation and independent whole-reply review flow above. Transcription is not a verified medical fact: check terms, negations, numbers and units, and correct anything misheard.

For spoken replies, the server sends only eligible, approved reply text to OpenAI's fixed speech model. A voice preview sends a fixed sample. Marin is the default, with Cedar, Coral, Sage and Ash also selectable. The voice is AI-generated, not a human recording. Automated review is not clinician approval or an accuracy guarantee. Speaking during preparation or playback stops obsolete local audio; the server attempts to cancel obsolete work, but already-started requests may still incur charges. The complete reply stays in chat, and a conservative, client-reported completed-audio boundary helps prevent subsequent coaching from assuming an interrupted remainder was presented. Playback status does not prove that you heard or understood the reply.

Microphone pre-roll and audio are held briefly in bounded memory, not saved in app data, exported backups or recordings. The app does not use audio for model training. OpenAI's own processing and retention policies apply separately. Study text, accepted review records and bounded request metadata are saved. Muting stops microphone capture; ending the session, leaving Coach, signing out, the browser reporting that it is offline, backgrounding the page and the ten-minute limit stop capture and discard pending audio. There is no hidden background listening. Microphone permission and actual playback quality must be checked on supported devices.

The app sends Ingenium Applicatum model, token counts, latency, completion time and estimated cost for each successful text AI call, including generation and review, so the operator can compare model usage. This feed excludes prompts, answers, review claims and excerpts, audio, email addresses and user identities. Usage metadata is retained in Ingenium separately from your study conversations; the operator controls its retention and deletion. Ingenium's current receiver does not ingest speech requests. Speech usage and cost are left unknown when the audio response supplies no verified usage record; text metadata alone is not a complete account of voice costs.

Browser speech recognition, when available and used, may process dictation through your browser’s speech service. In the Android app, use your phone keyboard’s microphone; that keyboard has its own privacy settings. Premium read-aloud needs a server connection and supported audio playback. There is no automatic device-voice fallback. If autoplay is blocked, Play audio resumes the fetched audio without another provider request.

Keep cases fictional or de-identified

Use this app for education. Do not enter identifying patient information, medical records, or information you are not permitted to share. The current private pilot is not presented as a HIPAA-compliant clinical service.

Cookies, devices, and backups

A session cookie keeps you signed in. The installable browser app caches its interface files; it does not cache chat or account API responses through its service worker. Chat and saved progress require a server connection.

You may export a JSON backup containing your study content and history. Keep this file private. Deleting an account does not remove copies you previously exported or copies retained outside this app.

Retention and deletion

Saved study content remains until you remove it or, in account mode, delete the account. Account deletion removes your account credentials, study workspace, stored answer reports, and stored coaching content. Anonymous accounting records and purchase fingerprints may remain for usage accounting and purchase-reuse prevention; the stored purchase token is removed. Google Play and AI-provider records are controlled separately by those services.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Read the deletion instructions and manage your subscription in Google Play separately.

The personal app uses a private access token instead of individual accounts. Its owner controls the server’s saved data and backups; individual commercial account deletion does not apply to that mode.
