Family Medicine Study CoachACCOUNT CONTROLS

Delete your study account.

You can request deletion from the app or from this website by signing in and confirming deletion in your study preferences.

Account deletion does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play separately.

How to delete

  1. Open your study account and sign in using the email and password you registered.
  2. Open Study preferences using the settings button. Choose “Delete my account”.
  3. Export a backup first if you want to keep a private copy of your learning.
  4. Read the deletion message, type DELETE, and choose “Permanently delete account”.
Sign in to delete my account

What deletion removes

Your account credentials, authentication sessions, saved cards, review history, conversations, preferences, stored answer reports, and stored coaching content are removed. The stored Google Play purchase token is removed.

Anonymous usage-accounting records and purchase fingerprints may remain to account for consumed service and prevent purchase reuse. Data held by Google Play, the AI provider, your device’s keyboard or speech service, and exported backups are outside this account deletion operation.

Manage a subscription

Use the Google Play subscription controls inside the Android app, or open your Google Play subscriptions. Confirm cancellation there if you want to stop renewal. Deleting study data and canceling a subscription are separate actions.

Personal app or sign-in problems

The personal private app uses an access token and does not have individual study accounts. Contact the person who provided your app link about clearing its server data or backups.

The operator’s public contact is not configured. Account-recovery support is not active for this private test. Ask the person who provided your test access for help. Public release remains blocked.

Operator and public support details must be completed before wider distribution.
