# Incorporated study material

Inventory and official-source currency check: **October 10, 2026**. This describes the checked-in material supplied to a new study space; personal cards, restored backups, and subsequent coaching conversations can add to it.

The current phone pilot contains **117 disease conditions, seven additional study topics, six separate Foundations of Care topics, 566 original board-style questions (546 disease/topic plus 20 foundation questions), 12 fictional reflection cases, 12 starter recall cards, and six skill-reflection areas**. The new condition library uses original short teaching summaries and bounded permitted passages linked to checked sources. Each question has five choices, a canonical answer, an explanation, explanations for the other four choices and supporting sources. These are original educational items, not official ABFM questions. Source checks are automated editorial work; independent clinician review and a clinical accuracy benchmark remain pending.

Open **Library → Guidelines & boards** to search conditions, read sections, inspect source editions/check dates/jurisdictions and answer questions. Save a question to **Review** for spaced repetition or open its condition in **Coach** for source-supported follow-ups. The text RAG selects current known reference sections; generated factual replies require an independent whole-reply source check. Unsupported details are withheld. Premium audio reads approved study text; canonical browser speech remains available where supported. Unchecked generated Realtime speech is disabled. The whole app is labeled **study use only, not medical advice or for clinical use**.

The bank combines original cardiometabolic/renal/urologic, respiratory/infectious/GI, neurologic/psychiatric/MSK/skin and reproductive/pediatric/hematologic/urgent teaching with later treatment and permissive-document expansions. New lifespan, immunization, geriatric, eye and sports modules address selected gaps. Of the 117 disease conditions, 106 include formal guideline or official recommendation sources and eleven have official-reference coverage only. The U.S. practice pool has 558 questions; eight comparative-library items are excluded. See the generated [condition inventory](CONDITION_COVERAGE.md), [bank manifest](../content/board-bank-manifest.json), [remaining gaps](CURRICULUM_GAPS.md) and source-audit reports for exact editions, scope, permissions and clinical-review limits. No count establishes an exhaustive syllabus.

Today, Progress and finished board sessions now show learning priorities derived from retained trusted attempts. Optional confidence, first exposure and repeats remain separate. The learner can review a cited rationale, try a different same-topic item, save a recall card and return to mixed practice. ## Live reference finding and source comparisons

The existing RAG supplies answer evidence from current permitted passages. **Library → Guidelines & boards → External reference directory** adds all 75 catalog/document entries, searchable by acronym and specialty. Its explicit topic search finds MedlinePlus title/publisher/topic links; each publisher also offers an outward Google site search. This is reference discovery, not automatic reading of protected websites. A link or page title cannot support a medical fact or quiz answer. Search sends the topic to NLM; clicking Google sends topic/domain to Google. Use study terms only, without patient details. Results show the submitted topic and fetch/cache time; publication date remains unknown unless actually supplied. The free NLM endpoint is cached for 12 hours with bounded requests, timeout and no automatic retry. Actual hosted connectivity remains pending.

When approved replies cite multiple sources, **Compare cited source context** exposes editions, dates, jurisdictions and available limitations. The generation/review policy requires both supported positions and citations when recommendations differ, with population/setting/edition differences separated from genuine conflicting guidance. It cannot infer agreement from missing text. These gates do not guarantee detection of every conflict.

Generated medical assessment questions are withheld unless served from the supported canonical bank. Neutral teach-back can refer only to an exact supported point already taught, while ordinary conversation and study planning remain available. See [the resource matrix](source-audit/RESOURCE_INCORPORATION_STATUS.md) for exact imported material, blocked sources and remaining commercial-rights work.

## Monthly official-source maintenance

The maintenance interval is **once each calendar month**, with urgent safety changes reviewed as soon as identified. A scheduled source check and subsequent clinical approval are separate steps. See `content/source-checks.json` for the current observations and `docs/CLINICAL_CONTENT.md` for eligibility and release requirements.

The owner's scheduled task **Refresh Family Medicine sources** was created and enabled on October 9, 2026, then expanded to cover the full condition corpus. It starts November 1, 2026 and repeats on the first day monthly at 08:00 America/New_York in the configured morning window. It checks actual condition-specific official sources, replacements, editions, addenda, withdrawals, safety notices, conflicts and reuse policies. Verified source-supported original updates to the personal pilot can be published/deployed after affected checks, with clinician review still pending. Blocked or uncertain material is quarantined and reported. It does not approve the commercial corpus or authorize app-store publication. Task reference for operators: `6ac87ed8329c81918c3701660444bf86`.

1. Check the official ABFM blueprint/current booklet, AHRQ teach-back page, all condition-specific and Foundations of Care sources and applicable source/rights pages. Follow official replacement links and check final published editions, addenda, effective dates, withdrawals, endorsement changes and safety notices. Record unavailable or incomplete pages as blocked, not current. Do not refresh dates merely because a URL responds.
2. Compare those observations with the previous monthly manifest. Record the source URL, check date, displayed edition/date, affected app content, and the proposed action. Preserve enough editorial evidence to reproduce the comparison; do not ingest protected full text into a public repository or an AI corpus merely because it can be read online.
3. Correct affected original personal study material only where actually verified permitted official evidence supports the change. Preserve study-only labels, jurisdiction and pending clinician review. Automated research cannot supply clinician approval or grant a content license. A qualified clinician must verify claims, populations, exceptions, contraindications and conflicts, and rights approval must cover the proposed use before commercial activation.
4. Withhold affected published teaching records while a clinically important conflict or change is unresolved. Expired rights and review dates already make guideline records ineligible. Record corrections or withdrawals, preserve learner review history, and make revisions visible; do not overwrite personal cards silently.
5. Run only checks affected by changed content, source metadata, prompts, retrieval or models. Regenerate the integrity manifest/coverage after a completed corpus revision; use the explicit `--allow-quarantine` maintenance mode only when current evidence gaps require honest partial-coverage reporting, and validate changed foundation records separately and update the validation ledger. Publish verified personal educational revisions through GitHub and the existing private Railway pilot, observing deployment SUCCESS. Keep commercial approval separate. App-store submission still requires the owner's explicit permission.

The initial source checks expire November 9, 2026. If monthly maintenance fails or evidence is unresolved, the retrieval layer stops treating expired material as current. The bank covers selected teaching points, not every dosage, exception or newly published recommendation. Formal-source gaps and excluded documents appear in the audits and app. The registry's October 9 USPSTF landing-page error and CDC/AAFP immunization conflicts remain separate observations; no blanket immunization schedule has been silently imported or adjudicated.
