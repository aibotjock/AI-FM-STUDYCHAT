## Source and population boundaries retained

- Immunization uses the operative **July 2, 2025** CDC notes identified by the source audit; an October 2026 check is not a 2026 guideline edition. The HPV initiation/immunocompromise distinction and maternal-HBsAg-positive newborn prophylaxis qualifiers remain visible. No new COVID-19, RSV or seasonal influenza regimen was inferred.
- CDC STI recommendations retain their **2021** edition and relevant correction. New gonorrhea oral-drug FDA approval status is kept separate from CDC treatment priority; a regulatory news release does not establish a complete dosing or first-line guideline. Weight thresholds, route, duration, pregnancy and partner/timing qualifications are not generalized beyond the selected chapters.
- VA/DoD pregnancy remains its **2023** source. The severe postpartum hypertension review anchor is within 72 hours **after hospital discharge**; the other hypertensive-disorder anchor is 7–10 days **after delivery**. Different anchors are deliberate. Weak recommendations and VA/DoD-specific scope are preserved.
- OA uses the 2026 full guideline; LBP uses the 2022 edition and its actual current recommendation table, rather than the differently worded 2017 comparison entries. Renal risk, selected knee-OA duloxetine use and pre-replacement injection timing retain their source qualifications.
- NEI/NIAMS educational references support the imported broad recognition and treatment concepts; they are not represented as professional ophthalmology or sports-medicine management guidelines. CDC developmental checklists remain surveillance/conversation aids rather than diagnostic tests. Falls material remains scoped to community-dwelling older adults and does not defer evaluation of acute injury.
- Exact-document reuse proposals and exclusions are documented by their authors. No blanket permission for linked society articles, borrowed tools, images or contractor material was inferred. This review does not itself grant commercial rights.
