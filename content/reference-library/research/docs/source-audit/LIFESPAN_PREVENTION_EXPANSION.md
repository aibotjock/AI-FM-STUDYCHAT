## Explicit omissions and review queue

- **Vaccines:** excluded from this author's batch. The operative CDC schedules must be pinned independently; the date on a schedule must not be replaced solely because the calendar year changed. Root owns the separate vaccination work.
- **Comprehensive newborn care:** no HRSA RUSP document or newborn treatment source is imported here. Screening panels do not establish complete newborn treatment or well-child care.
- **AAP Bright Futures and protected instruments:** not imported or AI-processed. Existing permission requirements remain unchanged.
- **Clinical review:** all 24 questions still need qualified medical/editorial review for interpretation, ambiguity, educational discrimination and U.S. board relevance. Automated authorship/source inspection is not clinician approval.
- **Quality limits:** these questions are original application exercises; they have no psychometric calibration and are not claimed to predict ABFM scores or professional competence. Scope expansion is measurable, while completeness and commercial publication remain unestablished.
