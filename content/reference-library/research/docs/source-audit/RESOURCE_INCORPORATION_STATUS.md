## Rights decisions before expanding retrieval

1. Prioritize exact current CDC/MMWR, VA/DoD and eligible NIH/NLM/NCI text with usable diagnostic/treatment depth. Record population, recommendation strength, correction status, original title/link, date, permitted reuse and supporting source spans.
2. Use only the selected MedlinePlus text/feed fields; exclude vendor articles, drug monographs and images. Do not automatically follow and ingest external links.
3. For HIV, verify current versions against the public-domain publication notice. For PDQ, distinguish quoted evidence summaries from complete NCI products and from formal treatment guidelines.
4. Keep SAMHSA TIP material out of paid retrieval until exact fee-distribution conditions are resolved. [TIP 26](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK571030/), [TIP 39](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK571081/) and [TIP 63 publication notice](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK535268/pdf/Bookshelf_NBK535268.pdf) provide concrete barriers, not blanket government clearance.
5. Seek explicit commercial AI rights for protected specialty material only where needed; specify retrieval, embeddings, external-provider processing, subscriber text/audio, original questions, corrections, territory and termination. No permission request or contract has been executed.
6. Use exact CC0/CC-BY documents where clinically appropriate; NC/ND, access subscriptions, fixed quotation limits and training-only permissions are insufficient for unrestricted paid RAG and derivative teaching.
7. Keep provenance, rights and question support as separate requirements. Unsupported diagnosis, treatment, antimicrobial selection or next-step content remains a recorded gap; a source list alone cannot make an unsupported tutor question answerable.

## Verification limits

Rights pages were reviewed as primary official sources or official indexed policy text. The registry notes direct fetch failures and unresolved policies rather than claiming complete access. Source-publication dates, website reuse policy dates and clinical freshness are separate. These findings are a conservative engineering incorporation review, not executed permissions or an assurance that every publication from a listed organization is commercially reusable. The parent integration owns document import, clinical review, regression validation, publication and deployment.
