# Women's health, pediatrics and other high-yield conditions: source audit

Checked **2026-10-09**. Content expires **2026-11-09** unless reverified. This file documents the 25 records in `content/conditions/women_children_prevention.json`: 76 concise teaching sections and 50 original five-choice application questions. These are independent educational questions, not copied ABFM items, ABFM-endorsed questions or clinically approved patient-care guidance. All records retain `humanReviewed: false`.

The material is for **study only and is not medical advice**. It is a bounded learning curriculum, not a complete treatment protocol, a prevalence-ranked list or a replacement for the official publications. Formal guideline, official recommendation and official reference labels are distinguished below and in each source record. No inaccessible full-text recommendation was used as a keyed-answer source.

## Access gaps, conflicting details and source status

- **ACOG:** Direct opens of Committee Opinion 557, Committee Opinion 785, the endometriosis FAQ, the March 2026 diagnosis guideline and its February 2026 press release failed, usually with a 402 response. AUB and ectopic summaries use only facts verified in their accessible primary ACOG abstracts. The inaccessible documents are not JSON evidence citations. No full-text access, current reaffirmation or clinical review is implied.
- **Endometriosis:** Search located the new 2026 ACOG diagnostic guideline, but the guideline, official February announcement and primary publication abstract were inaccessible. This module uses accessible NIH treatment information and explicitly does not represent the new diagnostic algorithm. The [official ESHRE 2022 PDF](https://www.eshre.eu/-/media/sitecore-files/Guidelines/Endometriosis/ESHRE-GUIDELINE-ENDOMETRIOSIS-2022_1.pdf) and current official guideline page actually opened; diagnostic recommendations 5–7 and progestogen recommendations 15–17 were read. Its page 192 notice restricts use to personal/educational purposes and expressly denies commercial use and reproduction without permission. No ESHRE content or source citation was added to the runtime corpus. The US formal-guideline coverage gap remains. Independent audit identified an overbroad contraception statement: the section and first question now explicitly require an appropriate contraceptive formulation; menstrual suppression alone does not ensure contraception, matching NIH pregnancy precautions.
- **B12:** Canonical NICE NG239 recommendation and PDF URLs were blocked by 403/tool access errors; NCBI Bookshelf presented a challenge page. A third-party Pernicious Anaemia Society PDF mirror actually opened and displayed NICE NG239 and March 6, 2024, but it is not an official-host verification and the separate NICE AI/commercial rights requirement was not satisfied. No NICE recommendation, threshold or source citation was imported. ASMBS official micronutrient resource page opened but exposed no usable guideline text; the guessed full PDF failed. An accessible current US formal condition-specific guideline was not established. The record is an NIH reference fallback.
- **PCOS:** The old `prod.asrm.org` PCOS page returned an actual 404 page. The current `www.asrm.org` page successfully opened and uses the PMOS name while retaining the 2023 guideline. Its recommendations remain an international collaboration including ASRM. The source notes a noncommercial Creative Commons license and separately reserved figure rights; no figure, algorithm or full text is copied.
- **Cervical dysplasia:** The official ASCCP guideline PDF opened successfully; the PMC copy presented a challenge. The current ASCCP management index identifies the risk-based/enduring-guideline framework. This record only teaches the cited foundational HPV16/18 and long-term surveillance recommendations, not all subsequent test-specific updates.
- **BV:** The CDC 2021 page still contains a partner-treatment paragraph based on older evidence. This audit does not verify it as the latest recurrent-BV policy. That topic is omitted from the teaching sections and questions; initial diagnosis and antimicrobial choices are the explicit bounded scope. Do not expand into partner recommendations without checking later official updates.
- **AAP status:** The September 28, 2026 AAP index separates current, in-development and under-review/expired guidelines. Browser text did not expose the dropdown membership needed to certify the 2014 bronchiolitis guideline as current. UCSF 2024 is therefore the operative source. The expired/retired national UTI approach is not cited or used.
- **UCSF croup:** The March 2026 page contains an older `<3` epinephrine-treatment discharge criterion in one section and a newer FAQ stating there is no evidence-based dose-count threshold mandating admission. The curriculum excludes dose-count disposition rules entirely and uses persistent stridor/work of breathing and sustained response, which are consistently supported. No numerical dosing dispute is imported.
- **Iron deficiency:** Gastro.org guideline and news pages were blocked; the accessible AGA educational recommendation page and primary technical-review caption directly support both keyed answers. No unverified ferritin cutoff is imported. H. pylori timing differences between summary/full guidance are outside the question scope.
- **US relevance:** The PCOS and pressure-injury sources are international collaborations with named US professional society participation, recorded as international in `jurisdiction`. No NICE-only rule is presented as a US recommendation. UCSF sources are explicitly institutional consensus, not national directives.

## Reuse and editorial boundaries

Only brief original teaching summaries and original hypothetical questions are stored. Source links, titles, visible versions and section locators are retained; scraped documents, exam items, figures and lengthy source passages are excluded. Each single-source condition has fewer than 175 words across its overview, teaching sections, question stems, correct options, keyed explanations and learning objectives before the independent incorrect alternatives. Clinical distinctions in distractor notes are short; there are no verbatim source quotations. Reused pages appear in one condition only, except paired sources for the same condition. No commercial reuse permission is inferred from a public URL, NIH hosting, a source-check date or an open-access label.

The two reference-only gaps are visible in record-level `limitations`. Other scope limits and the croup/BV excluded issues are also visible there. These modules do not populate the separate clinician-approved commercial corpus or remove release gates. Any new/changed guideline should trigger claim-specific review rather than silently promoting an automatically generated answer to clinical approval.
