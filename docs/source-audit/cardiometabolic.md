## Blocked publications, rights and unresolved coverage

JACC full2025 BP page returned403;2026 lipid DOI redirected to an inaccessible publisher page. Primary AHA/ACC recommendation summaries independently support the incorporated facts. Several ADA HTML pages returned403 and PMC exposed explicit restrictions on text/data mining and third-party reuse. ADA, AUA and IDSA bodies therefore **are not stored, chunked, embedded or used as the incorporated clinical source text**. Their existence/edition/access limitations are recorded here only; clinical facts for those topics use independent federal or permitted accessible primary support.

Several PMC pages presented a CAPTCHA; ATA hypothyroid/hyperthyroid publisher URLs timed out. The ATA nodule PMC full text and ACR society-hosted gout PDF were accessible. A former ACR PDF path returned an index rather than the guideline; the current ACR guideline page was followed to its actual hosted PDF. ASH journal links were inaccessible; ASH-hosted educational guideline resources were accessible.

This source check establishes page access and support for bounded original teaching, not commercial reuse permission. No protected guideline full text, tables, source exam questions or long excerpts are included. Source-derived facts are expressed in concise original sections, hypothetical vignettes and short explanations; linked official titles are retained for verification. The aggregate section/explanation/correct-choice budget is126words at most per opened page. Including every distractor explanation raises the maximum to170words per page. Original hypothetical patient scenarios and intentionally incorrect alternatives are not source summaries; counting their entire wording as well produces a maximum271words, so the narrower derivation accounting is disclosed rather than calling every case word a quotation. No source is used for a comprehensive rewritten guideline. BPH remains official-reference-only because the current AUA2026 body is restricted and an official CUA PDF could not be retrieved. Full US protocol/surgical coverage gaps require permitted source expansion and clinician review before marketing comprehensive clinical coverage.

No unresolved numerical contradiction was found within the bounded incorporated facts. Important differences are made explicit instead of merging sources: AHA2025 PREVENT BP threshold, AHA2026 lipid framework,2026 PE severity categories, international KDIGO jurisdiction, and2012 final versus2026 draft AKI guidance. A source check date is not a guideline publication date; unspecified publication dates remain null.

## Retrieval and assessment boundaries

Questions are independently composed single-best-answer application exercises, with five distinct choices, one keyed answer, explanations for each distractor, a bounded objective, and source/section references. They are not ABFM questions or endorsed exam material. Answer positions are balanced acrossA–E. Coverage is introductory and selective; two questions per condition do not establish full board-exam readiness.

The monthly updater should revisit the listed official hubs and condition-specific pages, detect new final editions and withdrawal/conflict signals, preserve the expired status until a successful supported revision, and route clinical changes for review. It must not activate a draft, mark `humanReviewed` true automatically, claim source permissions or alter commercial release gates.
