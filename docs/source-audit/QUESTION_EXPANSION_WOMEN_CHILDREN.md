## Reuse review

- CDC policy: most agency text is public domain, subject to third-party exceptions, attribution, preserved substantive meaning, nonendorsement and notice that the original is free. Only the specified agency narrative is used; no logos/images or external documents. New paraphrases do not change the recommendation meaning.
- NICHD policy: agency text may be freely copied with credit; externally copyrighted articles and images are excluded. NICHD diagnostic references can be older. Menstrual procedures are described without inventing indications/age cutoffs. Endometriosis imaging/selected biopsy is described without importing the older universal surgery-confirmation wording or claiming a modern mandatory-surgery pathway.
- NIDDK policy: most information is copyright free, with sponsored/graphic exceptions. Original summaries are educational; no logos, endorsement, individualized medical treatment or referral recommendations.
- NHLBI policy: generally public domain; no alterations to published formatted products or endorsement. Only original educational paraphrases are supplied, with no figures, formatted tables or numeric cutoffs from illustrations.
- NLM policy expressly makes health-topic summaries and medical-test information public domain. Only those components are used; A.D.A.M. encyclopedia material, drug monographs, images and linked third-party pages are excluded.
- NCI policy permits text reuse unless otherwise indicated, with title/link attribution; graphics and linked ASCCP calculators are excluded.
- NIH general copyright FAQ supports the government-authored ODS text, with copyright exceptions preserved. No externally authored tables or figures are imported.
- Article-specific CC BY 4.0 permits commercial sharing/adaptation with attribution, license link and change notice. Both licensed source records contain attribution and derivativeNotice outside the four-key rights object. No claim of blanket journal licensing is made.
