## Visible gaps and bounded coverage

- **BPPV:** reference-only NIDCD module on clinical pattern, history and particle repositioning. No formal BPPV guideline is claimed. The AAO-HNS guideline page was opened, but its displayed restrictions on transferring guideline content to applications or AI infrastructure prevented incorporating its material. Its 2026 quality measure is not a guideline and carries separate commercial licensing restrictions. Neither was imported, summarized into active content or used for question keys.
- **GAD:** reference-only NIMH publication, revised 2025, independently supports recognition, CBT and benzodiazepine tolerance/dependence counseling. No comprehensive current US formal GAD guideline was verified. A UK NICE supplement was considered and then removed entirely because AI/app reuse permission was unresolved.
- **Epilepsy:** CDC seizure first aid and ongoing self-management recommendations plus the MedlinePlus reference updated January 9, 2026. This is not comprehensive pharmacologic epilepsy guidance. The actual 2015 AAN first-unprovoked-seizure summary and its April 2015 release were read, but current reaffirmation/withdrawal status could not be established from the accessible official index; therefore it is excluded from the active corpus. NICE NG217 was successfully read through a public HTML request and displayed an August 5, 2026 update, but all NICE-derived sections, sources and questions were removed pending reuse permission.
- **Alzheimer dementia:** primary-care diagnostic evaluation using the DETeCD-ADRD guideline first published December 23, 2024. The official Association index also lists a July 2025 specialty-care blood-biomarker guideline; that different setting, anti-amyloid treatment eligibility and complete biomarker algorithms are not covered here.
- **Delirium:** general recognition from MedlinePlus, with AGS formal recommendations specifically for postoperative delirium in older adults. Do not generalize the guideline into a complete ICU treatment protocol.
- **Osteoporosis:** Endocrine Society recommendations specifically for postmenopausal women, including the 2020 update. Do not infer equivalent coverage for men, younger patients or all secondary osteoporosis.
- **Migraine:** acute treatment and recognition/red flags are sourced; this module does not claim to cover every preventive therapy or subsequent prevention-specific guideline.
- **Osteoarthritis:** active June 2026 VA/DoD hip/knee provider summary and September 14, 2026 ACR update summary were read. Questions are bounded to hip/knee/core care. Do not reuse obsolete 2020 claims about hyaluronic acid or supplements: the 2026 VA/DoD recommendations differ. The new ACR conditional methotrexate recommendation concerns erosive hand OA; no blanket claim that methotrexate never has an OA role is made.
- **Atopic dermatitis:** current official adult guideline highlights include the 2025 focused update; the April 7, 2026 first pediatric guideline summary was read separately. Agent-specific pediatric age approvals are not inferred from an adult recommendation or a class name.

## Access problems, excluded sources and conflict handling

- AAO-HNS BPPV guideline: https://www.entnet.org/quality-practice/quality-products/clinical-practice-guidelines/bppv/ (actual open `turn132view3`). The page displayed explicit app/AI-transfer restrictions. AAO32 2026 measure: https://www.entnet.org/resource/aao32-2026/ (`turn132view4`) displayed commercial licensing terms. Both excluded.
- NICE public guideline pages returned 403 through the web reader. Public HTML was available through an ordinary URL request for GAD, BPPV and NG217; these were read only during source evaluation. No NICE clinical material remains in the JSON after coordination identified unresolved AI reuse permission. No license approval is claimed. Rights-policy pointers supplied by the team: https://www.nice.org.uk/reusing-our-content/nice-uk-open-content-licence ; https://www.nice.org.uk/reusing-our-content/nice-syndication-api ; https://www.nice.org.uk/terms-and-conditions .
- NINDS migraine/epilepsy pages returned access errors; NIA Alzheimer treatment retrieval did not yield usable clinical content. Those unread pages are not cited as active evidence. MedlinePlus, CDC and successful primary guideline sources supplied the limited content actually incorporated.
- AAN epilepsy topic-directory access did not establish the current status of its 2015 first-seizure guideline. Its PDF remains available (`turn132view2`), and the publication date was confirmed in the actual official release (`turn180view2`), but availability alone was not treated as reaffirmation.
- PMC full-text access for Alzheimer/delirium sources was blocked or yielded a browser challenge. The publishing societies' actual Wiley guideline pages were read instead.
- The CDC stroke symptom page includes a broad three-hour treatment statement. The current AHA January 2026 stroke summary specifically supports eligible disabling stroke thrombolysis within 4.5 hours and selected extended-window care. CDC is used only for emergency recognition/urgency; all reperfusion questions use the current AHA recommendation. No unresolved timing conflict is carried into a question key.

## Reuse and question-authoring controls

Only concise original teaching summaries, original hypothetical scenarios, links and locators are retained. No full guideline text, PDF, copyrighted clinical instrument, table or exam question was copied into this module. Short source-derived facts are bounded; questions do not reproduce ABFM items. Reused protected pages are summarized briefly across related conditions, while federal references supply independent facts where possible. Source visibility and source availability do not establish commercial permission. The approved commercial corpus and release gates remain separate.

Every key is tied to a section and the supporting official source. Vignettes supply the relevant setting and exclusions where treatment choice could otherwise be ambiguous. Two questions per condition target separate concepts. Choice order was redistributed to exactly ten correct answers in each position A–E without changing the keyed text or its distractor rationale.
