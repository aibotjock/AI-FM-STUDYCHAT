# Respiratory, infectious and gastrointestinal source audit

Checked 2026-10-09. Dataset: `content/conditions/respiratory_infectious.json`.

This contribution contains 25 distinct conditions, 75 sourced sections and 50 original, hypothetical five-choice application questions. It is an independent educational contribution, not official ABFM content, a prevalence ranking, medical advice or a clinician-approved reference. Every record is labeled `humanReviewed: false`, checked on 2026-10-09 and expires on 2026-11-09. No clinical-review identity, commercial permission or guaranteed accuracy is asserted.

Source pages or recommendation text were actually opened/read. Search snippets were used to locate sources, not as the sole support for keyed answers. Official recommendations are distinguished from formal guidelines. AGA clinical practice updates are expert best-practice recommendations and are not mislabeled as systematically graded clinical guidelines. The IDSA CAP pathway is implementation guidance, not a newly published guideline.

## Access problems, rights boundaries and unresolved scope

- AAO-HNS official rhinitis page was opened (`turn101view2`) but its footer explicitly prohibits copying/transferring site content into third-party applications or AI infrastructure. Its content is excluded. Rhinitis uses the independent Joint Task Force publication; otitis externa uses explicitly labeled HSE guidance. A US clinician-reviewed otitis externa source with suitable reuse terms remains an editorial improvement.
- ATS's 2025 CAP update was identified and its official release opened (`turn107view0`), but the site also states third-party AI use is restricted. That content is excluded. New antibiotic-duration, corticosteroid and virus-positive decisions are not taught by these questions. The dataset's CAP source is honestly labeled the IDSA implementation pathway; it is not represented as the latest full CAP guideline. The current ATS/IDSA difference around the 2025 update remains outside this narrowly scoped module, not silently resolved by the app.
- GOLD's official 2026 report landing page was opened (`turn98view3`). Its pocket-guide PDF exceeded the browser retrieval size limit (`turn99view0`), and direct read failed with HTTP 403. No clinical recommendation was taken from the blocked PDF or a third-party mirror. The current US VA/DoD 2026 summary supplied COPD evidence instead.
- A browser-tool open of the VA COPD page returned a cached 2021 listing, while direct official HTTP 200 returned the new 2026 listing and exact new PDF links. Legacy PDF links returned 404. The actual 2026 provider summary was downloaded only for temporary text inspection and then deleted. The document's stated date is September 2026; October 7 is the observed posted-file date, not an invented publication day.
- AGA page opens sometimes returned HTTP 403 in the browser tool. Read-only direct HTTP requests returned 200 and exposed the actual recommendation text. Several PMC and PubMed alternatives returned CAPTCHA/cookie screens; those blocked copies are not used as evidence. The IBS publication abstract and diverticulitis abstract were successfully read through PubMed, and the corresponding recommendations were checked against actual publisher/society text where noted.
- Concise original summaries and hypothetical questions are retained with URLs and locators. No guideline PDFs, scraped pages, copied diagrams, tables or full text are stored in the app. Visible copyright and source access do not establish commercial licensing; that remains separate from source verification.
- The narrower coverage gaps in the table are deliberate boundaries. Retrieval should decline unsupported questions, including condition-specific doses/regimens outside the incorporated evidence, rather than fabricate missing guideline recommendations.
