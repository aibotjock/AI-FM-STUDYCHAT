## Selection and integrity

All passage words come from the publisher's article paragraphs. No medical statement was drafted to fill a gap. The selected text retains complete recommendations, their evidence grades where present, and the identified qualifications. Whitespace and quotation-block markers were normalized; typographic subscripts/superscripts became plain text, and inline linked bibliography labels retain their printed label text. Intervening unselected paragraphs are omitted; the locators identify which paragraphs were selected. App section titles and selection decisions are editorial additions.

Each section records the exact resulting excerpt, SHA-256 of its UTF-8 text, DOI, paragraph/recommendation locator, and captured publisher HTML line numbers. Line numbers are only inspection aids and may change with page layout. Source-span hashes cover the normalized selected excerpts, not the entire source document. Important preserved limits:

- Levothyroxine starting dose is contextual. The selection includes young/healthy full-dose context, coronary-artery disease low-dose treatment and the limited-duration hypothyroidism exception.
- TSH-guided adjustment retains central-hypothyroidism and measurement exceptions and patient age/comorbidity qualifications.
- Four-hour separation from interacting medicines is explicitly described as traditional but untested.
- Methimazole choice is not presented as universal. Recommendation 13's original exceptions remain intact; no pregnancy regimen or thyroid-storm regimen is supplied by this selection.
- The initial methimazole dose guide remains a rough guide with free-T4 ranges, individualized qualifications, and T3 monitoring; it is not an individualized prescribing tool.
- Early antithyroid monitoring retains the warning that TSH can remain suppressed and is not a good early parameter.
- Symptom-triggered blood/liver assessment is paired with the guideline's insufficient-evidence statements about routine monitoring.
- Graves duration/discontinuation retains normal TSH and TRAb conditions and relapse/longer-term-treatment options.

## Corrections, excluded material, and remaining scope

Two current hyperthyroidism correction notices were opened:

1. [2017 correction, DOI 10.1089/thy.2016.0229.correx](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1089/thy.2016.0229.correx): pediatric radioiodine recommendation 58.
2. [2025 corrigendum, DOI 10.1089/thy.2016.0229.correx2](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1089/thy.2016.0229.correx2): Table 6 thyroid-storm scale.

Their affected content areas are outside this selection. The current online original has been updated according to the notices; neither correction notice's prose is imported. Table 6 contains separately credited Burch/Wartofsky material, and is expressly excluded. All tables, figures, pediatric regimens, pregnancy-management sections, thyroid-nodule/cancer-management sections, brand marks, pocket cards, and other third-party material are excluded.

The app must use these as adult nonpregnant study references and must not infer unavailable regimens. Separate current, exact-document rights/source review would be required to add pregnancy, pediatric care, thyroid nodules/cancer or emergencies. The ATA catalog's 2026 pregnancy guideline and 2025 differentiated-thyroid-cancer guideline are not imported by this fragment.

Passage review expires November 9, 2026 and remains `humanReviewed: false`. Monthly source checks must inspect the exact article, new guideline/catalog changes, correction notices, and license status. The source check does not silently extend expired host condition review dates.
