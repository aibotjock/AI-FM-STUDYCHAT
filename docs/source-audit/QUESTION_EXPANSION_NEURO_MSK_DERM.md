## Content and item-quality decisions

- New q3/q4 items use different objectives from the preserved original q1/q2 items. Correct choices rotate across A–E, with ten additions in each position.
- Every addition has five choices, one keyed answer, a rationale, four specific distractor explanations, a support section, and an exact eligible government-text source ID. All alternatives stay within the topic’s differential, assessment, treatment, counseling, or realistic reasoning errors. An incorrect alternative is not an asserted medical fact.
- Explanations reject alternatives narrowly within the stated vignette. They do not infer new universal contraindications, dosing, or diagnostic criteria from absent evidence. No dose, unverified efficacy estimate, or unsupported clinical protocol is added.
- No formal evidence-review claim is made for NIH consumer education. In particular, new dermatology/RA/osteoporosis/CTS items focus on the aspects supported by the permitted agency text rather than reproducing restricted society treatment algorithms.
- The NIH News article contains expert discussion. Added text uses the newsletter’s declared agency article content in original paraphrase; no expert quote, linked research paper, or third-party expressive material is reproduced.
- VA/DoD adult guidance is used only within the cited scope. The acute back-pain stems specify nonpregnant adults; conditional OA/MDD recommendations are presented as suggestions, not universal requirements. Full questionnaires and instrument content are excluded.

## Outstanding scope and source gaps

This expansion does not turn the corpus into a fully clinician-reviewed board course. Additional licensed or appropriately rights-cleared formal specialty guidelines would still be useful for dermatology, dementia, RA, osteoporosis, CTS, and other domains. Restricted originals remain unchanged for the host’s existing trust/rights gate; their presence does not authorize commercial ingestion or support these new additions. No old source’s medical check date or expiration is renewed here. Monthly source maintenance and full-corpus budget checks remain host responsibilities.
