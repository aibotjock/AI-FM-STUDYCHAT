The rights basis is the exact agency policy plus an inspected original agency text page, not the presence of a government domain alone. No licensed images, animation, video, external linked publications, third-party tables, logos or society full text were copied. No protected exam items were used. Government-source attribution is recorded on every new source. Agency names and links must not imply endorsement. CDC content is otherwise available free on its source page; the study summary does not change its substantive recommendations. ## Coverage and unresolved limits

New objectives differ from the existing questions: measurement discrepancies; neurologic warning findings and first-repeat measurement; lipid mechanisms and combined lifestyle support; discordant diabetes testing and meter limitations; prediabetes surveillance and clinical testing; autoimmune diabetes classification; digestion-dependent rescue and delayed exercise lows; DKA prevention and delivery-failure physiology; thyroid diagnostic limits/pregnancy/structural tests; weight goals and self-monitoring; coronary test functions; atypical infarction and emergency transport; heart-failure monitoring; AF rate-control limits and underlying contributors; anticoagulant safety/filter limits; pulmonary test functions; complementary kidney tests/albumin confirmation; AKI prevention; stone imaging limits; urinary organism versus structural assessment; pyelonephritis severity/recurrence; BPH medicine aggravators/retention; and gout crystal assessment/urate-lowering mechanisms.

These are federal clinical references or educational explanations, not full formal CPGs. They do not resolve professional-society licensing gaps or supply comprehensive current pharmacotherapy, detailed risk thresholds, diagnostic staging, dosing, contraindication or inpatient protocols. Current access verification is distinct from publication freshness: several source pages retain 2016–2022 review dates, recorded where visible. Stable testing/safety principles are used narrowly; no assertion that the whole guideline bank is current or clinically approved is made. Exact month-only dates are retained as descriptive editions rather than invented day-level publication dates.

Question q3/q4 additions are not duplicated versions of old items; nevertheless educator/clinician review of difficulty and alternatives remains pending. The PE scan-function question is an application of test-purpose interpretation rather than a complete diagnostic pathway. The AKI additions address prevention and medicine safety rather than inpatient AKI treatment. These limits are intentional and are not filled with unsupported society material.
