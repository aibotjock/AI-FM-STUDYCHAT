## Focus on the new voice behavior

1. In foreground Android Chrome over HTTPS, open **Coach** and confirm the teal/gold conversation circle is visible. Tap **Start conversation**, grant microphone permission, and wait for the microphone indicator. The first target is Android Chrome; other browsers/native shells need their own acceptance. If capture is unavailable, use typed chat or keyboard dictation. A denied/disconnected microphone must leave typing reachable.
2. Say a short ordinary message such as “I have only 15 minutes today; can we plan my studying?” Pause naturally, including one brief hesitation. The app keeps local pre-roll, detects a completed utterance and uploads mono WAV to OpenAI transcription. Captions appear after the completed transcription; continuous cloud transcription and partial cloud captions are not implemented. Check for lost initial words or repeated text.
3. Let one reply begin, then say “Wait, keep it brief.” Check that playback stops and the new message is captured. Speak softly once and try a headset if available. Local VAD and requested browser echo cancellation are best effort; actual player PCM is not supplied to the optional correlation input. Speaker echo and credible short backchannels may interrupt. Use **Interrupt reply** or typing if detection is unreliable. Do not interpret a passed synthetic test as certified acoustic separation.
4. Continue with a follow-up and check context. The full earlier reply stays visible, but future voice context uses conservative completed audio chunks rather than assuming its unplayed remainder was heard. An interruption starts a new turn; it does not silently replay or automatically regenerate the old reply. Existing history and learning preferences support coaching; persistent-memory consent controls and editable pronunciation are not yet built.
5. Try **Mute microphone / Unmute microphone** once. Mute releases capture; unmute requests it again. **Stop conversation**, moving the app to the background, browser-reported loss of connectivity and logout should stop the microphone and audio. Coming back online must not start recording automatically. A provider/server error may retain local monitoring for manual recovery but must never automatically repeat a paid request. A disconnected microphone should display an actionable unavailable state.
6. If autoplay is blocked or playback stalls, use **Play reply/Play audio** to resume prepared audio. Watch actual preparing, starting, playing and buffering states. Approved MP3 can use MediaSource progressive playback where supported or buffered playback; this is not unchecked model speech. While audio plays, adjust the phone's **media** volume and check Bluetooth/headphone routing. Browser playback events do not prove audible output. Record the device/browser, approximate end-of-speech-to-first-audio wait and interruption-to-silence behavior; no measured latency gain is claimed yet.
7. Once ordinary conversation works, request one short family medicine study point, for example “Let's study atrial fibrillation; give one cited point and one recall question.” Check visible citations and qualifiers against the spoken content. Generated medical audio waits for the same independent whole-reply grounding and current-source authorization as text. Try an A–E bank question; a reflective follow-up must not reveal its pending answer without an explicit reveal. A withheld reply is not permission to bypass review or retry automatically.
