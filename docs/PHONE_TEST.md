# First phone test

Open https://family-medicine-phone-test-private-test.up.railway.app on your phone. In **Study access code**, paste the value of `STUDY_ACCESS_TOKEN` from Railway → `private-test` → `family-medicine-phone-test` → **Variables**. This is separate from `OPENAI_API_KEY`, which is already configured on the server. Do not paste the OpenAI key into the app.

The personal pilot uses OpenAI; Claude is inactive. It does not start a Google Play subscription or require a commercial account. The owner has already confirmed live phone sign-in, and one actual bounded OpenAI connection check passed. Coach now combines a context-aware conversational layer with the cited study bank. Actual phone speech remains to check. Use [VALIDATION_STATUS.md](VALIDATION_STATUS.md) to focus on outstanding checks; repeat a passing check only when a relevant change or failure justifies it.

Try the new conversational path first: in **Coach**, say or type “Help me plan a 15-minute study session.” Answer the Coach's follow-up, choose atrial fibrillation as your topic, then ask for one question at a time. Continue with “Can I have a hint?” before answering A–E. The question should remain pending, and grading should refer to that question. Ask a source-supported follow-up after the answer. Coaching uses recent history and saved learning preferences; medical teaching remains the exact cited bank text. Quoted learner statements are labeled unverified, and written clinical reasoning is not independently graded.

1. Open the link in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone. Unlock the study space if asked. Start in **Library → Guidelines & boards** to try the new 105-condition library: choose a condition, read its sources and limitations, answer a question and save it for Review. Then open that condition in **Coach** for cited follow-ups. Previously confirmed sign-in need not be re-tested unless it fails.
2. Open **Board practice** from Today or Library. Start a 20-question mixed block, answer a question, review its explanation and citations, save a missed question to Review, and try resume/results. In Coach, request “Quiz me on atrial fibrillation,” then answer A–E; the next reply grades that specific question. Written clinical reasoning is not independently graded.
3. Tap **Talk with Coach**, allow browser speech-recognition access, and say “Help me plan a 15-minute study session.” Reply to the follow-up, choose a sourced topic, and request a quiz. The recognized text is submitted to conversational study chat; browser speech synthesis reads the server-rendered coaching and eligible canonical teaching, with citations visible. Quoted learner reflections stay visible but are excluded from trusted spoken text. Answer an option, interrupt once, try Mute/Unmute and Stop. Sessions end after ten minutes or when the page enters the background. Verify your actual phone permission, recognition, audible playback and interruption. If browser recognition is unavailable, use typed chat or keyboard dictation. Unchecked Realtime speech is disabled; no extra audio model is used.
4. After stopping voice, use the separate app dictation microphone to fill the composer. Speak a short sentence, pause and continue; check that revised recognition text does not duplicate words. Review before sending. Your keyboard's microphone is another option. The unvalidated native Android shell still uses keyboard dictation and has no realtime microphone permission.
5. Pinch to zoom and drag the page horizontally and vertically. Reset zoom and confirm the composer and bottom navigation are reachable. Local Chromium native-touch checks passed; your phone's gestures remain to confirm.
6. Try **Read aloud** on a current conversational Coach reply. It should read only trusted server-rendered coaching and current cited teaching, excluding learner quotations. Historical, imported or uncited generated replies are not eligible for sourced playback. Speech support depends on the device/browser. Confirm you can stop playback by tapping Read aloud again.
7. Save a useful learning point as a card. Write a focused question, check its answer, and add a reference when it contains a medical fact.
8. Open **Review**. Attempt your answer, reveal it, then choose an honest Again/Hard/Good/Easy rating. Check **Progress** to see the completed review.
9. Open the fictional reflection cases and try conversational reflection prompts. They do not invent medical findings or assess clinical judgment. Choose **Coach → History** to return to your first conversation, then continue the topic. Reload to check that your study content remains saved.
10. In **Study preferences → Choose or test a model**, choose an available OpenAI text model and press **Use selected model**. Try **Test model connection** once for a model without a saved result, then **Export model results**. A new connection check is a paid API request capped at 512 billed output tokens. Completed results are reused. The `gpt-4.1-mini` connection has already passed; avoid paying to repeat it. Conversational dialogue-plan calls record their selected/returned model. Direct canonical quizzes, grading and fixed offline prompts do not require a model call and therefore need no invented model metadata. Astra is prohibited with no exceptions; do not configure or test it.
11. In Study preferences, export a study backup. Install the browser app through your browser’s Add to Home Screen/Install app option if desired.

Use fictional or de-identified cases. The app is for study only, not medical advice or for clinical use. Source-linked summaries/questions have automated editorial source checks and pending independent clinician review; formal guideline gaps and non-US jurisdictions are labeled. Text Coach and spoken study share source-linked teaching, with learner quotations excluded from trusted speech. Conversational wording is constrained by validated server-rendered acts. Source selection can still be incomplete or irrelevant; an attached citation alone is not medical adjudication. The finite question bank is not a complete syllabus or passing guarantee. Educational feedback is not a formal competency evaluation. Phone functionality and model connection checks do not validate clinical accuracy. Ingenium's metadata feed is connected; routing remains direct to OpenAI. Keep the access code private: everyone with it can access the same workspace and its owner model controls. See [STUDY_MATERIAL.md](STUDY_MATERIAL.md) for exact coverage and monthly source maintenance.

## Separate commercial preparation mode

Commercial mode is optional and separate from the personal pilot. Private pilot registration may require an invite supplied by the owner. The free private pilot is not a paid subscription trial. Subscriber model selection is not enabled. The checked-in reviewed clinical corpus is empty, so commercial clinical coaching abstains until eligible reviewed content is supplied.

Google Play pricing is shown only from actual Play product details in the installed Android app. A purchase unlocks coaching only after server verification. There is no local subscription simulation. Do not test live billing until the owner has configured the product, eligible offers, and a Play license-testing account.

Privacy and account-deletion disclosures are available at `/privacy.html` and `/account-deletion.html`. In commercial mode, the website deletion link lets you sign in, review the deletion message, and confirm with DELETE. Account deletion and Google Play subscription cancellation are separate actions.
